Gospel Wonders

A Church Built Around Faith.

Gospel Wonders Mission Church is a Christian church located in the Bulenga Gogonya area of Wakiso District, Uganda.

Our desire is to provide a place where people can come together in worship, prayer, fellowship and the Word of God.

We welcome people from different backgrounds and invite everyone to discover a deeper relationship with God and grow together as a community.
